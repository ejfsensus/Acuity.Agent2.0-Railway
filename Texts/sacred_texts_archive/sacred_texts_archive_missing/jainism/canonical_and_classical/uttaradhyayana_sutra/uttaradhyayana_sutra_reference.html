Note: The Uttaradhyayana Sutra is contained in Sacred Books of the East Vol. 45, Part 2. This volume also contains the Sutrakritanga Sutra. Together with Vol. 22 Part 1, these two volumes contain the major SBE translations of Jain canonical texts.

Notes

The Uttaradhyayana Sutra is one of the most important Jain scriptures. It contains 36 lectures on various aspects of Jain doctrine and practice, delivered by Mahavira and other Tirthankaras. The text covers topics including the soul, karma, liberation, asceticism, and moral conduct.
