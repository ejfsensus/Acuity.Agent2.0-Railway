Qolasta (Qolastā) - The Mandaean Canonical Prayerbook

Archive Note

The Qolasta (also spelled Qolastā) is the canonical prayerbook of the Mandaeans. On gnosis.org, this text is presented under the title "Ginza Rba" (also spelled Ginza Rabba), which translates to "The Great Treasure" or "Great Book".

The Qolasta and Ginza Rba refer to the same collection of Mandaean prayers and liturgies. The full text has been downloaded from gnosis.org and is available at:

  • Ginza Rabba (Ginza Rba) - Full HTML
  • Ginza Rabba (Ginza Rba) - Plain Text

About the Qolasta

The Qolasta is the principal collection of Mandaean religious poetry and prayers, containing the baptism liturgy, masiqta (death mass) prayers, daily prayers (nishmata), hymns of praise, and other liturgical texts. The version on gnosis.org is excerpted from:

E.S. Drower, Canonical Prayerbook of the Mandaeans, Leiden: 1959
